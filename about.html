Hi, I'm Dev.

I founded SARVAYA in 2024 after a few years of shipping production websites, AI agents, and automation systems for startups and agencies across India, the United States, the United Kingdom, and Australia. The work that paid the bills early on was unglamorous - migrating clunky WordPress sites, debugging broken Stripe integrations, writing the kind of automation scripts that turn three hours of manual ops into a one-click button.

What I learned doing that work shaped how SARVAYA operates today. Most agencies confuse motion with progress: more meetings, more status updates, more decks. We try to do the opposite - ship fast, ship visibly, and let the working product be the conversation. A 24-hour website. A live AI agent that handles a customer-support inbox. A blog post that ranks because it actually answers a real question. Outcomes that the client can point at, not slides about outcomes.

Today the studio runs out of Jaipur with a tight team, a network of vetted partners, and a roster that mixes direct client work with white-label production for agencies in three time zones. We are deliberately small. The founders work on the work, not just on the company. If you hire SARVAYA, you get me on the project, not a sales handoff to a junior account manager.

What I write about

I publish on this blog two or three times a week. The topics I keep coming back to are SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (how content gets cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews), the practical economics of running a small agency, AI tooling for shipping faster, and the few opinionated takes I have on web performance and accessibility. The writing is not theoretical - it's whatever I am dealing with on client work that week. If you find a post that is wrong, email me.

Where to find me

The fastest way to reach me is email. For project work, the contact page or WhatsApp gets a same-day reply. For agency partnerships and white-label production, see the white-label page for how we structure those engagements.
